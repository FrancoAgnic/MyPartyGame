[h1]Sculpturillo v0.9.3 - Level Creator[/h1]

[h2]Workshop & Maps[/h2]
[list]
[*] The Workshop browser now has a Maps section: search, subscribe to and download community maps, right next to word packs.
[*] The official map now shows up as a selectable option in the map library.
[*] Map and word-pack thumbnails now appear in Game Settings and in the library (with a default image when an item has no thumbnail of its own).
[/list]

[h2]Gameplay & UI[/h2]
[list]
[*] Before each turn, guessers now see the theme (map + word pack) while the sculptor picks the word, with a smooth pop-in and fade-out.
[*] The darkest color you can pick is now a dark gray instead of pure black, so the sculpt/paint preview stays readable through geometry.
[*] Head sculpting: the brush preview now shows darker when it's behind or inside the head, matching the main sculpting mode.
[*] Menus and panels no longer briefly flash in English before switching to your language.
[/list]
