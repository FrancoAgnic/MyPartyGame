TITLE: v1.0.8 - Share your skins on the Workshop

[h2]Sculpturillo v1.0.8[/h2]

[h3]New[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Skin Workshop[/b]: from the Locker you can now open a Skin Workshop panel to browse skins published by the community, download them and equip them (and edit them afterwards like your own). You can also publish your current skin (head + body) straight from there.
[/list]

[h3]Fixes[/h3]
[list]
[*]The color picker no longer stutters the first time you open it in Head Sculpt (and it's warmed up quietly in the background).
[*]When you open the color picker, the cursor now starts centered on the color wheel.
[*]Fixed the background sometimes showing a wrong (red/grey) material in a freshly created skin thumbnail.
[/list]
