TITLE: v0.9.4 — Level Creator polish: bake hotbar, memory budget, smarter erase & exact eyedropper

[h1]Level Creator update (v0.9.4)[/h1]
[b]Building maps is smoother, cleaner and easier to control.[/b]

[h2]Bake & hotbar[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Bake slot with progress ring[/b] — hold Enter to cook an asset; a ring fills over the 3 seconds and shows "Model saved!" when done.
[*][b]Environment (F) slot[/b] added to the hotbar automatically.
[*]Color picker and Environment slots now light up while active.
[/list]

[h2]Erase, rebuilt[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Erase by contact[/b] — hold and sweep to delete every prop the brush actually touches (mesh-accurate, not bounding boxes).
[*][b]Highlight[/b] — props about to be deleted glow red.
[*][b]Reach control[/b] — scale the brush up to its max, then keep scrolling to push it out and erase from far away.
[*]The erase brush now lands on any asset, even ones outside the play area.
[/list]

[h2]Performance (LOD)[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Distance-based detail[/b] — props far from the map's center are drawn with lighter, decimated meshes in 3 configurable stages, keeping big maps fast.
[/list]

[h2]Quality of life[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Memory budget bar[/b] — see how much the map uses as you place props; it blocks placing once the limit is reached.
[*][b]Player spawns[/b] are shown while building so you don't bury them under props.
[*][b]Exact eyedropper[/b] — copying a color from a prop now gives the identical color, no ambient tint.
[*]Sculpting the box floor no longer flips you out of Sculpt mode.
[*]Level Edit mode is cleaner: only the tools that apply are shown, and it always starts on Add.
[*]Match HUD no longer shows Level Creator-only widgets.
[/list]
