BBCODE (paste directly into the Steam patch notes / event description box):

[h1]✨ What's New[/h1]
[list]
[*] 🧊 New 3D depth grid: a crisp volumetric grid of fine lines appears around your brush so you can read how deep you're sculpting in mid-air.
[*] 💡 Contact glow: when your brush touches the existing clay, the grid lights up brighter right at the contact point.
[*] 🟥 Boundary shading: the edges of the sculpting box are highlighted with a soft radial shadow, so the limits of the play area read clearly.
[/list]

[h1]🎨 Improvements[/h1]
[list]
[*] 🎯 The grid scales with your brush size (with min/max limits) so it never disappears or floods the screen.
[*] 🌫️ Smooth radial fade: the grid is solid at the center and fades out toward the edges — clean and unobtrusive.
[*] 🧭 Kept the single bottom height stick for ground reference; removed the extra axis rods for a cleaner view.
[/list]
