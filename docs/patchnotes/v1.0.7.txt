TITLE: v1.0.7 - See what off-screen players are saying

[h2]Sculpturillo v1.0.7[/h2]

[h3]New[/h3]
[list]
[*]Chat from players you're [b]not looking at[/b] (off-screen or behind you) now pops up as a little bubble at the edge of the screen, pointing in their direction — so you never miss what someone said. If you can see the player, it still shows above their head as before.
[/list]
